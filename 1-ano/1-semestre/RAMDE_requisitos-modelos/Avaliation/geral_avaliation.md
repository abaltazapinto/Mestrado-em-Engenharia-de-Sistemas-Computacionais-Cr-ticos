The course has 3 evaluation components: M1 (40%), M2 (40%) and M3 (20%).

All components relate to evaluation during the semester, but M1 is done during the classes, while M2 and M3 are done at the end of the semester (week 11).

For M1, and to be able to evaluate all students, 4 to 5 students were randomly selected between weeks 5 and 10, as presented in [M1 Evaluation Schedule Ficheiro.](https://moodle.isep.ipp.pt/mod/resource/view.php?id=83200 "https://moodle.isep.ipp.pt/mod/resource/view.php?id=83200")

Please consider this schedule. If, you are not able to attend your date, please try to switch with a colleague. If this is not possible, please contact me.

Also, the velocity of the project development is not an evaluation criteria. What is important is that you are able to discuss your project as it is by the time of your session and are able to perform the development activities during the 20 to 30 minutes of the session.
